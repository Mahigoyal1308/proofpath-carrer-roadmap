## Questions

1. What kind of role, if any, have you prepared for recently? (Optional short answer)
2. When you prepare for a job or internship, what steps do you usually take? (Open response)
3. How do you decide which skills or topics to practise for a particular role? (Open response)
4. What, if anything, makes that preparation difficult or time-consuming? (Open response)
5. How do you keep track of examples or work you might discuss in an interview? (Open response)
6. Think about the most recent time you prepared. What part, if any, felt useful? What made it useful? (Open response)
7. What would you change about the way you prepare? (Open response)
8. Is there anything else about job or internship preparation you would like to share? (Optional open response)
